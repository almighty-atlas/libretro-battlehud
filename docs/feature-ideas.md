# Suggested next features

These are proposals, not implemented features. Priority reflects usefulness for
playing on a small handheld screen and the profiles currently available.

1. **Hidden Power type and power** on the party/move page in Gen 2/3. Show the
   actual DV/IV-derived values, avoiding trial and error when choosing a moveset.
2. **Nature bonuses in Gen 3**: mark the raised/lowered stats with small arrows;
   show ability alongside the training values without hiding existing information.
3. **Training progress**: EV total/remaining budget in Gen 3 (total is already
   displayed), per-stat effective contribution and optional gains since the last
   battle. Gen 1/2 need their own stat-experience/bonus formula, without modern caps.
4. **Battle type icons and effectiveness for Gen 1/3**: separate verified battle
   profiles and generation-correct charts. Gen 3 must account for abilities,
   immunities and special move rules before making definite predictions.
5. **Overlay preferences**: toggle type icons, move hints and training tables
   independently; choose compact/detail modes and retain normal frontend controls.
6. **Catch helper**: a compact catch-chance estimate for the chosen ball, current
   HP/status and generation-specific rules, with uncertainty clearly represented.
7. **Useful party details**: friendship (Gen 2/3), Pokérus/EV multiplier (Gen 3),
   shiny status and gender where relevant, with generation-specific definitions.
8. **More ROM profiles**: Gold/Silver, Yellow, Ruby/Sapphire and FireRed/LeafGreen,
   then German editions, each gated by exact checksum and tested menu layouts.

Suggested order: Hidden Power, Gen 3 nature bonuses, independent HUD toggles,
then verified battle support for Gen 1/3. Expand edition/language profiles when
there is a concrete ROM and frontend available for acceptance testing.
